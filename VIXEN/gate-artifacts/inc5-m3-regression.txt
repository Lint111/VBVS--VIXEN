TUNING DECISION
----------------
amortizationFactor: 1 -> 8 (shipped default). Reasoning: M2's live re-measurement
found F=8 unanimously cheaper than F=1 across every re-run pairing (direction
confirmed real), and the edit-loop convergence gate proved F=8 still converges
correctly with a bounded ~8x longer wall-clock window, not staleness or
non-convergence. F=8 chosen over the also-gate-proven F=4 because M2's own
cross-check subset showed cost still monotonically decreasing at F=8 (6.14ms @F=1
-> 3.22ms @F=4 -> 2.15ms @F=8) with no sign of having hit the fixed-overhead floor,
and because F=8 is one of the plan's own two tested points -- banks more of the
confirmed reduction without extrapolating past what was actually measured (e.g. an
untested F=16+).

raysPerProbe: UNCHANGED at 64. M2's own joint bench had the raysPerProbe=16/32
columns backwards (a confirmed session-load artifact, not real regression data,
per gate-artifacts/inc5-m2-amortization-bench.txt), and no separately-clean raw
per-raysPerProbe-value table from Inc4 M6 survives in-tree to justify a specific
new value (M6's own bench log/artifact was never committed to the repo; only a
single secondhand cross-reference data point at raysPerProbe=64, +28.4ms, survives
inside the M2 artifact's own text). Changing raysPerProbe now would be tuning from
noise, not data -- left unchanged per the plan's own explicit permission to do so
and document it as a deliberate choice.

Grid density (countX/Y/Z): UNCHANGED at 8x8x8=512 probes. No data this increment
motivated a change; out of scope without a specific finding driving it.
