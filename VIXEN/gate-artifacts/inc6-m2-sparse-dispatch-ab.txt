CONCLUSION -- stated plainly, per this program's own honesty discipline
================================================================================
DOES the sparse-dispatch mechanism (M1) close the sub-linear gap toward the naive 1/F
prediction? PARTIALLY, and in the DIRECTION this increment predicted, but the evidence
is not strong enough this session to call it definitively closed:

  - The F1-vs-F8 direct comparison is UNANIMOUS (3/3 round-pairings) in showing F=8
    cheaper than F=1, with a savings magnitude (4.78ms mean) that is, if anything,
    LARGER than Inc5's own old-mechanism measured savings at F=8 (3.99ms, from
    6.14->2.15ms) -- consistent with the sparse-dispatch mechanism eliminating real
    per-workgroup overhead that the old dispatch-all/early-out-inside mechanism paid
    for every skipped probe, as this increment's own root-cause hypothesis predicted.

  - However, this session's own numbers do NOT land at or near the naive 1/F
    prediction either. Naive 1/F would predict F=8's cost at 6.14/8=0.77ms (a
    5.37ms reduction from F=1's 6.14ms baseline). This session's measured F1-vs-F8
    reduction (4.78ms) is closer to that 5.37ms naive-reduction target than Inc5's own
    3.99ms was, but still falls short of it -- i.e. the gap between measured and naive
    is SMALLER now (4.78 vs 5.37 naive = 0.59ms shortfall) than it was under the old
    mechanism (3.99 vs 5.37 naive = 1.38ms shortfall), roughly HALVING the shortfall,
    not eliminating it.

  - This means there IS still a non-trivial fixed-overhead component even after
    eliminating per-workgroup dispatch overhead for skipped probes -- plausibly the
    PushConstant/descriptor binding and ProbeGridConfig SSBO read that happen once per
    dispatch call regardless of workgroup count, or GPU/driver-side dispatch-launch
    fixed cost that doesn't scale down with workgroup count the way the plan's own
    root-cause hypothesis assumed. This is a real, non-obvious finding: the fix (M1)
    demonstrably helps (unanimous direction, larger absolute savings than the old
    mechanism, smaller shortfall from the naive prediction) but does NOT fully explain
    away the sub-linearity Inc5 first observed -- something beyond per-workgroup
    overhead still contributes.

  - Given this session's own noise profile (F4 in particular showing a session-level
    ambient spike in round 3, and the "off" baseline reproducing its already-known
    unusable quirk), this MAGNITUDE conclusion (roughly-halved shortfall) should be
    treated with the same caution Inc5's own magnitude findings were treated --
    directionally trustworthy (F=8 unanimously cheaper, savings larger than before),
    magnitude not fully precision-confirmed under this specific session's ambient
    conditions. A future cleaner re-measurement (quieter machine session) could sharpen
    this number further, but is not required to accept this milestone's own gate --
    the mechanism swap's CORRECTNESS was already fully gated in M1, and this session's
    A/B data is sufficient to state the "partially closes the gap, doesn't fully close
    it" finding with reasonable confidence given the unanimous direction signal.

HEADLINE: Sparse-dispatch (Inc6 M1) measurably helps beyond what Inc5's dispatch-all
mechanism achieved at the same amortizationFactor values (larger F1-vs-F8 savings this
session, 4.78ms vs Inc5's own 3.99ms vs-off savings), and moves closer to the naive 1/F
prediction than the old mechanism did (shortfall roughly halved: 0.59ms vs 1.38ms).
It does NOT fully close the gap to the naive 1/F prediction -- a real fixed-overhead
component beyond per-workgroup early-out cost still appears to exist. Reported as a
genuine, non-obvious finding, not smoothed over: the fix worked in direction and
partially in magnitude, but the underlying assumption that per-workgroup dispatch
overhead was THE dominant fixed cost is not fully supported by this data -- something
else (per-dispatch-call fixed cost, e.g. descriptor/push-constant binding or driver-
side submission overhead) plausibly still contributes and would need its own
investigation to close further.
