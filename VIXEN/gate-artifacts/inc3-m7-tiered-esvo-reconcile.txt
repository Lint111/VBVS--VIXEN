WHAT IT DOES (read+understood, not guessed)
---------------------------------------------
The tier-crossing LOD gate (decides whether to descend into a childScale-times-
smaller child tree, or shade the parent leaf's own mip sample instead) previously
compared `tv_max` (the LEAF's own exit-t, chord-floored for any interior ray --
Task 20's finding) against `childScale*scale_exp2`. At deep childScale ratios
(2^-10) the chord floor made the gate permanently declined regardless of camera
distance (Task 22's algebraic proof: the depth term cancels from both sides).

Task 23 fixes the gate's DISTANCE argument to be genuinely camera-anchored and
expressed in true world units at every hop:
  tChild           = along-ray t of the child cube's center (childOriginLocal,
                      clamped to [t_min, tv_max]) -- replaces tv_max.
  kPhys            = true-world distance per t-unit at this hop (==1.0 at hop 0).
  worldDistToChild = tWorldBase (hop-composed camera distance) +
                      (tEntryWorld + tChild) * kPhys.
  childWorldSize   = childScale * scale_exp2 * tLocalUnitWorld (keeps the
                      existing RHS semantics, expressed in world units).
  decline iff worldDistToChild*raySizeCoef + raySizeBias >= childWorldSize.

Two new hop-threaded floats (tWorldBase, tLocalUnitWorld) thread through the
wrapper's hop loop and into each call of the (per-hop) traversal body. A
companion fix: a taken crossing whose child tree then misses (or a
hop-budget-exhausted chain) now falls back to the deepest parked crossing
leaf's own mip sample (shadeFromMipSample) instead of a whole-ray sky miss --
gated on real mip coverage, so mip-less crossing scenes stay byte-identical.
