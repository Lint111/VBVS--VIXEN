## Why a shadow ray (design note, since it came up)
Direct lighting = BRDF·radiance·cos·**V**, where V∈{0,1} is visibility. Inc0 left V≡1 (light passes through everything). The shadow ray *is* V, evaluated exactly. It is NOT replaceable by photon mapping: photon mapping pays the same visibility cost in its light-tracing pass and buys *indirect* effects (caustics, bounce) on top — it is parked as optional/future in the design. ReSTIR (Inc3) does not remove the shadow ray either; it chooses *which* light to spend each shadow ray on. So Inc1's shadow ray is the exact-visibility primitive ReSTIR and DDGI are later built upon. **Camera-frustum / camera-importance biasing of ray budgets belongs to emission-style layers (Inc4 DDGI probe rays, any future photon layer) — NOT here**, because shadow rays are already camera-driven (one per visible pixel per light) and thus inherently frustum-local; recorded as a forward hook in the design doc §7.

## Self-Review
- **Why is Task 1 (refactor) its own milestone with a byte-identical gate?** The `TraceWorld` seam is load-bearing for the whole program AND for the RT-core spec (its G0). Proving the extraction is behavior-neutral in isolation means every later change is attributable — a shadow bug in M4 can't be a hidden traversal regression from M1.
- **Why keep shading in the march shader through M3, then move it in M4?** Two byte-identical gates (M1 refactor, M3 buffer round-trip) bracket the restructuring so the ONLY milestone that changes the image is M4 (shadows) — the same "isolate the visual delta" discipline that made Inc0's regressions attributable.
- **Why measure in M5 and not just eyeball shadows working?** The measured per-ray cost is a *deliverable*, not a nicety — the design doc's §6 explicitly defers the frame-budget split "after Inc1's measured ray costs." Without it, Inc3/Inc4 budgets are guesses.
- **Deliberate non-scope:** no reservoirs (Inc3), no temporal accumulation (Inc2 — so shadows will be hard-edged and, with >1 light later, noisy; that's expected and fine at 1 light), no probes (Inc4), no VNDF consumption (the sampler sits unused until Inc3/Inc5), no soft/area shadows (one shadow ray = hard shadow; area lights are a later concern), no HW-RT backend (RT-spec, separate).
- **Camera-frustum bias (user question):** correctly deferred — shadow rays are already frustum-local (one per visible pixel). The lever applies to Inc4 probe-ray / future photon emission budgets; noted in §7 of the design doc as a forward hook, not implemented here.
