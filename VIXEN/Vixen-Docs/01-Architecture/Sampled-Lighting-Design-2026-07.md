# Sampled Lighting — Program Design (2026-07)

**Status:** DRAFT design — program not scheduled; increments not planned in detail.
**Grounding:** Two adversarially-verified deep-research passes (2026-07-10; 3-vote claim verification, ~24 confirmed claims each). Verified findings are cited; anything marked *synthesis* is design inference, not verified fact.
**Siblings:** `RT-Core-Optional-Acceleration-Spec-2026-07.md` (the optional HW accelerator for this program's rays — the `TraceWorld` seam is shared infrastructure), `Lazy-Procedural-Delta-Baseline-Design-2026-07.md` (lighting rays see the resident set; probe hysteresis absorbs deltas).

---

## 1. Where lighting stands today (code-grounded 2026-07-10)

- `shaders/Lighting.glsl`: **one hardcoded directional light** (`normalize(1,1,-1)`), constant ambient 0.3, Lambert diffuse 0.7, Blinn-Phong specular with roughness-mapped exponent (`mix(64,4,roughness)`). The file's own header says "Can be extended with PBR or other shading models in the future." That future is this program.
- `shaders/BodyInstanceRayMarch.comp` (~1350 lines): primary rays march per-instance octrees, produce `hitColor`/`hitNormal`/`hitT`, and shade **inline** with `computeLighting(...)`. No hit record survives the pass.
- **Normals:** SDF bodies already produce gradient normals (`sdfGradientStored` central-difference in `StoredSdf.glsl`; `sdfGradient` in `SdfRecipes.glsl`). Binary/brick DDA hits produce axis-aligned face normals from the step mask. Mip-sample shading (`shadeFromMipSample`) carries its own normal.
- **Materials:** the live body path reads per-voxel **color + roughness semantic channels** from the SoA channel pool (Stored-SDF Inc3 contract) — roughness already modulates the specular today. `Materials.glsl`'s ID→color table is legacy (old grid scene types).
- **Absent:** shadow rays (nothing occludes the light), light data model, emissive anything, indirect illumination, temporal accumulation, denoising.

The gap between this and the research's target is the program.

## 2. Research grounding (condensed; verified 3-0 unless noted)

**The recipe:** keep the estimator skeleton unbiased where unbiasedness is cheap; buy speed with *explicitly declared* bias in the reuse machinery; route all residual error into one named bias budget (temporal accumulation + denoiser).

| Verified finding | Source | Consequence here |
|---|---|---|
| VNDF microfacet sampling has per-sample weight F·G2/G1 bounded in [0,1] — no BRDF-term fireflies; 2023 spherical-cap variant is a faster drop-in | Heitz JCGT 2018; Dupuy & Benyoub HPG 2023 | The default BRDF sampler, GLSL + CPU mirror |
| Per-pixel roughness/normals should parameterize the ray *distribution* (GGX lobe, specular elongation) and the ray *budget* (half-res + neighbor reuse ≈ 4× fewer rays; error = correlation not energy loss) | Stachowiak, SIGGRAPH 2015 (Frostbite) | Material channels drive sampling; resolve decouples shading rate from trace rate |
| Roughness-driven Tmax cuts are safe **because** the result is denoised; needs an environment/probe fallback | Khronos hybrid-rendering best practices (Wolfenstein: Youngblood) | March-distance budgets keyed on the roughness channel |
| ReSTIR: 6–60× equal-error unbiased DI vs prior many-light sampling; biased variant 35–65× (darkening near occluders); optimized ~100×. Unbiasedness is **conditional** — naive integration reuse silently adds uncontrollable bias unless MIS/contribution weights are carried across every reuse domain | Bitterli et al. 2020; Wyman et al. SIGGRAPH 2023 course | The DI backbone; the weight discipline is a hard rule, enforced by CPU-mirror invariant tests |
| DDGI probes: HW-RT-independent by design (probe-marched rays allowed; authors preferred one batched coherent dispatch), Chebyshev visibility in 16×16 RG16F octahedral per probe (statistical leak prevention, no hand placement), hysteresis absorbs dynamic content with zero invalidation machinery; kB/probe | Majercik et al. JCGT 2019; RTXGI SDK seam | The indirect-diffuse layer, probe rays traced through our ESVO |
| VCT: leak-by-construction is primary-source-admitted (anisotropic mips only *contain* it; 1.5× non-leaf memory); the field left SVO storage over *update* cost, not render quality — a pressure that doesn't apply when the ESVO **is** the scene | Crassin 2011; VXGI GDC15; Q-Games CEDEC 2014 | VCT demoted to optional coarse AO/glossy-fallback against sparse-mip mips; never primary GI |
| SPPM-style shared statistics are biased-but-consistent (bias → 0 in the limit); refuted 0-3: that 2013 GPU photon gathering hit real-time budgets | Hachisuka & Jensen 2009; Mara et al. I3D 2013 | Optional future layer; bricks are natural SPPM regions. Not in the core roadmap |

Key open **empirical** question (not answerable from literature): the cost of ~10⁵–10⁶ probe/shadow rays through VIXEN's ESVO traversal on the real GPU. Measured in Inc1/Inc4 gates; it sets every budget after it.

## 3. Target architecture

```
primary march (unchanged contract)      →  HitRecord buffer (albedo, roughness, normal, t, instance)
                                            │
direct:   light list → RIS/ReSTIR reservoirs → shadow rays via TraceWorld() ─┐
indirect: DDGI probe volume (probe rays via TraceWorld(), Chebyshev, EWMA)   ├→ shade (GGX+Lambert, VNDF)
specular: VNDF reflection marches via TraceWorld(), roughness Tmax           ─┘
                                            │
temporal accumulation (reproject/EWMA; converge-when-static)  →  [denoiser, later]  →  tonemap/UI
```

Structural decisions:

1. **Split hit from shade.** The primary march stops shading inline and writes a `HitRecord` (G-buffer-equivalent for a ray-marcher: albedo, roughness, world normal, hitT/world pos, instance id). Lighting passes are separate compute dispatches consuming it. This is what makes every subsequent layer attachable — and it's PassGroupNode/auto-sync-shaped (each pass a `ComputeStageNode`, declared hazard edges, one submit group).
2. **One traversal seam.** All lighting rays go through the `TraceWorld()` / `TraceWorldShadow()` include defined in the RT spec (§7 there). Backend 0 (software ESVO, factored from the existing traversal) is this program's implementation; backend 1 (ray query) arrives independently via that spec. The factoring is gated byte-identical (RT-spec G0) and lands **here**, in Inc1, since shadow rays are its first consumer.
3. **BRDF upgrade, not addition.** `computeLighting`'s Blinn-Phong is replaced by Lambert + GGX (Smith, single scatter) with the VNDF sampler as its importance sampler — one BRDF used by both the analytic direct term and the stochastic layers. The existing roughness channel maps directly; a metalness channel is optional later (channel pool is semantic and extensible by contract).
4. **Lights become data.** A `LightingConfig` + light array (directional / point / emissive-body) authored as a canonical `[GpuStruct]` schema → generated C++/GLSL via the config-codegen pipeline (drift-guarded, like `OctreeConfig`). Default content = today's single directional light, so Inc0 has zero visual delta by construction. Emissive-as-content (an emissive semantic channel feeding the light list) is a format decision deferred to the ReSTIR increment — VIXEN owns the format; UNDERSET stays a consumer.
5. **Normals policy.** SDF gradient normals where the provider supplies them (already true), face normals otherwise; probes and diffuse shading tolerate face normals, specular quality gates may motivate per-brick normal channels later (content decision, not architecture).
6. **Bias ledger.** Every layer declares its error class up front:

| Layer | Estimator status | Declared error mode |
|---|---|---|
| VNDF/GGX sampling | unbiased | — (radiance-term variance remains) |
| ReSTIR DI (unbiased mode) | unbiased — **SHIPPED Inc3, 2026-07-12** | variance near shadow edges (confirmed via M4/M6 equal-error-vs-brute-force gates, 0.044%/0.15%); **biased mode (35–65×) explicitly DEFERRED, not silently dropped** — `ReservoirConfig.biasedModeEnabled` exists as a scaffolded flag, stays 0, no biased-mode shading logic was written this increment |
| Shadow rays | exact visibility vs resident set | lazy-procedural non-resident content invisible (shared with primary rays — consistent image) |
| Half-res + neighbor reuse (specular) | biased | correlation, no energy loss |
| Roughness Tmax cuts | biased | dropped distant reflectors → probe/env fallback required |
| DDGI probes | biased, temporally consistent | lag (hysteresis window); thin-feature leaks (Chebyshev caveat) |
| Temporal accumulation | consistent when static | ghosting when moving, handled by camera-motion reprojection + per-pixel validation (Inc2, shipped 2026-07-11); **honest caveat:** M4's validation uses a color-consistency reject sound for Inc2's deterministic march but flagged (KI-023) to fight Inc3's stochastic sampling — Inc3 must swap in a worldPos/depth geometric reject before enabling noisy sampling, or accumulation is silently defeated at high-variance pixels |

Everything not in this table is required to stay unbiased. New shortcuts must be added to the ledger or rejected — this is the enforcement of the research's "bias is a chosen knob, not an accident" rule.

## 5. Engine integration

- **Passes:** each lighting stage is a `ComputeStageNode` in a PassGroupNode; hazards (HitRecord buffer, history images, probe atlases, reservoir buffers) declared → auto-sync bakes barriers/timeline edges. No new sync machinery needed until the AS resource class (RT spec §9).
- **Pass-graph shape = fan-out/fan-in, realized by honest hazard declaration (not hand-parallelized).** The direct-visibility term and the indirect-radiance term read the same scene (octree/bricks + HitRecord) but write DISJOINT outputs and neither consumes the other — so they are genuinely independent passes that fan out from HitRecord and fan in at a final `shade = direct + indirect` combine. Because they share no read-after-write hazard, the auto-sync scheduler emits them with NO barrier between (and, on queues that support async compute, one pass can overlap the other's memory-stall latency — a real but hardware-dependent, modest win, NOT a literal 2× since a single full-screen dispatch already saturates the GPU). The key architectural point: this parallelism is a *property of declaring I/O honestly*, not something to hand-code — declare disjoint outputs and the scheduler already does it. Caveat on what is actually independent in THIS roadmap: ReSTIR DI (Inc3) is the direct pass with the shadow ray INSIDE it (not a parallel sibling); the genuine independent sibling is the DDGI probe-update pass (Inc4), which updates in world space on its own cadence while the shade pass merely samples the probe field; a future SPPM photon layer would fan out the same way. Inc1 has a single lighting pass — nothing to parallelize yet; the shape matters from Inc4 on.
- **Configs:** all lighting constants through `[GpuStruct]` canonical schemas (drift-guarded parity tests, per-field offsets — the `SpirvReflector` nested-struct support already exists).
- **SDI:** new bindings (HitRecord SSBO, probe atlas images, reservoir SSBOs, light SSBO) flow through existing reflection/DescriptorResourceGatherer machinery; no hand-written descriptor code.
- **Verification:** live-run gate authoritative (memory rule — static review passed runtime bugs repeatedly); CPU mirrors via gpu-shader-debug for every sampler/estimator kernel; ablation gates vary exactly one factor (recipe-epic lesson); lavapipe for structure, real GPU for perf/budgets, Windows-native for any Tier-1 runs.

## 6. Open decisions

1. **Emissive content**: semantic emissive channel — **DECIDED + SHIPPED at Inc3 (2026-07-12): a SCALAR-RGB emissive channel + mip-cut light-tree**, not an app-side light list (format change, enables light-from-voxels and destructible lights). **KEY (2026-07-10):** emission averages UP the sparse-mip ESVO → a million glowing voxels become a bounded CUT through the emissive mip tree (a light-tree for free, reusing LOD structure) — so emissive-as-light is NOT a million-area-light problem; the mip-cut feeds ReSTIR reservoirs (Inc3, shipped) + DDGI gathers (Inc4, future). Self-glow (voxel adds its own emission) is trivial/near-free. **Deferred fast-follow, DELIBERATE (not forgotten):** temperature→blackbody-spectral emission on top of this proven scalar-RGB reservoir machinery — user-chosen LAYERED sequencing (Inc3 plan Self-Review) so a reservoir-math bug is never tangled with a phenomenological-spectral bug. Fuller direction incl. a distance-kernel spectral-photon "good-enough wave" gather: `Spectral-Emission-Photon-Direction-2026-07.md` (exploratory, on the OPTIONAL photon layer, needs its own research pass — does NOT fork this spine).
2. **Probe placement** in sparse/mostly-empty worlds (uniform grid wastes probes in vacuum): **DECIDED + SHIPPED at Inc4 (2026-07-13): uniform grid** (`ProbeGridConfig` — world-space origin/spacing/counts, 8×8×8=512 probes default), exactly as originally scoped. Octree-anchored/cascaded placement is confirmed STILL DEFERRED, not silently dropped — Inc4's own frame-budget finding (§6 #4 below: shipped defaults don't fit 60fps) makes grid-density reduction a live near-term lever anyway, and cascaded/octree-anchored placement remains the longer-term answer for sparse worlds; interacts with Tiered-ESVO scale ambitions (a galaxy has no single probe grid) — revisit when tiers meet lighting.
3. **Denoiser**: SVGF-class vs lighter bilateral + heavier accumulation; ReSTIR correlation-blob behavior is the deciding test (Inc5).
4. **Frame-budget split** at 16.6 ms (primary march + direct + probes + specular + accumulate): set after Inc1's measured ray costs, not before. **FIRST DATA POINT (Inc1 M5, 2026-07-10):** ~240 ns per shadow ray through the ESVO traversal, measured on real GPU (Windows-native, AMD Radeon) at 1080p via an A/B lever (`ShadowConfig.enabled=1` vs `0`, same scene/camera, frame-time delta / hit-pixel-count) — full method, raw numbers, and signal-quality check in `gate-artifacts/inc1-m5-shadowray-cost.txt`. Caveats that matter for sizing later budgets: (a) the number is CPU-full-frame-delta-derived, not an isolated GPU-dispatch timestamp — `GPUQueryManager`'s isolated-timing path is currently non-functional in this graph (KI-019), so the number includes some CPU/present overhead the intended method would have excluded; (b) the equivalent 1440p measurement was INCONCLUSIVE (delta 0.78× the run-to-run noise floor — present/vsync jitter dominated at that resolution/hit-count) so no resolution-scaling trend can be claimed yet; (c) the test scene's hit-pixel count is very sparse (~0.24% of frame — a small object against mostly sky), so the per-ray cost may not hold for denser real content. Treat 240 ns/ray as an order-of-magnitude anchor for early Inc3 ReSTIR ray-count and Inc4 probe-budget sizing, not a precise constant — re-derive with the isolated-dispatch method once KI-019 is fixed and against denser scenes before finalizing any hard budget. **SECOND DATA POINT (Inc2 M5, 2026-07-11):** temporal accumulation (converging-1/N EWMA path) costs ~1.2 ms/frame full-frame delta at 1080p on the same machine/method (`VIXEN_ACCUMULATION_ENABLED=1` vs `0`, static camera, same KI-019 CPU-FrameTimer substitute) — signal MODERATE (2.34, not clean; accumulation's own run-to-run variance up to 1.17 ms is comparable to the delta itself, more repeat runs would tighten this). Equivalent to ~0.6 ns/pixel over the FULL frame — note this is a DIFFERENT cost shape than the shadow-ray number (per-pixel-blend vs per-hit-ray-cast; different denominators, don't sum naively). Reprojection's incremental cost over plain accumulation could not be isolated with a static-camera A/B (its branch is dead code without camera motion) — deferred, needs a moving-camera lever. Full method, numbers, and signal-quality discussion: `gate-artifacts/inc2-m5-accumulation-cost.txt`. **THIRD DATA POINT (Inc3 M7, 2026-07-12):** ReSTIR (RIS + temporal + spatial reservoir reuse combined, `DirectLighting.comp`+`SpatialReuseShade.comp`) costs ~5.4 ms/frame full-frame delta at 1080p on a ≥10³-emissive-voxel scene (`VIXEN_RESERVOIR_CONFIG_ENABLED=1` vs `0`, same KI-019 CPU-FrameTimer substitute, same scene held constant via `VIXEN_RESTIR_GATE_DEMO=1` on both sides of the A/B) — signal MODERATE-WEAK (1.56, weaker than both prior data points; a shared upward drift between run-1 and run-2 pairs on BOTH configs suggests shared-machine background load during the measurement window rather than a ReSTIR-specific effect, but this further widens the already-wide error bars on this number). Roughly 4.5× Inc2's accumulation cost — qualitatively consistent with RIS's multi-sample-per-pixel cost (candidateCount=8 + a shadow ray per selected sample + temporal combine + spatialCount=4 neighbor combines) exceeding a single shadow ray's, though this measurement could not isolate a clean per-ray figure the way Inc1's did (hit-pixel count was captured on a different app/resolution than the timing runs; RIS+temporal+spatial cost doesn't cleanly decompose into one "cost per ray"). Full method, numbers, and signal-quality discussion, plus a real SkyProjectionNode boot-blocking regression found+fixed while dispatching these runs (RenderGraphNodes → per-node OBJECT-lib split silently dropped the M1-era `VIXEN_SHADER_SOURCE_DIR` define): `gate-artifacts/inc3-m7-restir-cost.txt`. **FOURTH DATA POINT (Inc4 M7, 2026-07-13):** the DDGI probe-update pass (`ProbeUpdate.comp`, spherical-Fibonacci ray cast + light-tree single-sample direct-lit radiance + hysteresis blend into the irradiance/visibility atlases) at its SHIPPED DEFAULT (`raysPerProbe=64`, 8×8×8=512-probe grid) costs ~24.1 ms/frame full-frame delta at 1920×1080 on the DDGI leak-gate demo scene (`VIXEN_PROBE_GRID_CONFIG_ENABLED=1` vs `0`, same KI-019 CPU-FrameTimer substitute, 2 runs/config × 11 stable windows/run) — signal 19.3, the CLEANEST of any measurement in this program to date (vs Inc1's 5.64, Inc2's 2.34, Inc3's 1.56), and independently cross-validated by Inc4 M6's own separate bench session (~20-21ms/~28.4ms-at-raysPerProbe=64 via a different-length-run methodology) landing in the same ballpark. Full method, numbers, and the M6 cross-check: `gate-artifacts/inc4-m7-ddgi-cost.txt`. **FRAME-BUDGET FINDING, STATED PLAINLY:** at the 16.6ms (60fps) budget, this cost ALONE exceeds the ENTIRE budget by itself — twice-independently measured (M6 and M7, two different bench sessions, two different methodologies, same conclusion). **The current Inc4 shipped defaults do NOT fit a 60fps frame budget.** This is not a minor caveat to note in passing: it is the load-bearing conclusion of this increment's own cost measurement, and it means grid-density reduction, ray-budget reduction, and/or an async/amortized per-probe update schedule (M6's own Progress Log lists these as the live options) are REQUIRED before any production use of DDGI at these settings — a decision explicitly left open for a future increment, not resolved here. This is a COST finding, not a correctness one: DDGI's architecture (fan-out/fan-in sync shape), leak rejection (Chebyshev, ablation-verified), and edit-loop convergence (byte-identical EWMA match) are all independently verified sound by M3-M6's own gates. **FIFTH DATA POINT (Inc5 M2/M4, 2026-07-13):** the amortized per-probe update schedule (§4 Inc5 above) was the first lever exercised against the fourth data point's finding. M2's own interleaved re-measurement (F=1 vs F=8, 4 pairings, quieter session than M4's own re-check below) found F=8 unanimously cheaper than F=1 in every pairing — direction confirmed real, magnitude explicitly NOT precision-tunable from that session's own data (M2's own caveat). M4's own re-measurement this milestone (tuned-default F=8 vs Inc4-original F=1-override vs fully-disabled, 3 runs/config, `gate-artifacts/inc5-m4-ab-measurement.txt`) came back noisier still — pooled amortization-delta signal 1.24 (weaker than every prior data point in this list except Inc3's own 1.56), direction agreeing in only 2 of 3 run-pairings, and the "disabled" baseline again showing its already-known non-zero-cost quirk (a per-workgroup early-out, not a pass-removal — cost cannot be isolated against it cleanly, consistent with M2's own finding, not a new problem). **Net effect on the fourth data point's own finding, stated plainly: IMPROVED, not RESOLVED.** A real, confirmed-direction cost-reduction lever now ships defaulted ON (`amortizationFactor=8`), but no session in this program has yet produced a clean, precisely-quantified magnitude for the reduction, and `raysPerProbe`/grid density remain at Inc4's original values (deliberately untuned this increment, M3). The probe-update pass's cost at the shipped default has NOT been shown to fit inside a 60fps budget — that remains an open question for a future increment, now starting from a cheaper (if imprecisely-quantified) baseline than Inc4 shipped.
5. **Body motion vectors** for reprojection (instance-transform reprojection covers rigid bodies; procedural deformation doesn't) — scoped at Inc2 planning, DEFERRED at Inc2 M5 close-out (2026-07-11): Inc2 shipped camera-only-motion reprojection only. Rigid-body instance-transform reprojection remains a later increment (candidate: whenever body-motion accumulation artifacts are prioritized against ReSTIR/DDGI); procedural deformation stays out of scope entirely (no general solution proposed).

## 7. Forward hooks (noted, not yet scheduled)

- **Camera-importance / frustum biasing of ray budgets.** Where a layer *emits* rays not tied to a visible pixel — DDGI probe rays (Inc4), any future SPPM photon layer — the emission/gather budget can be steered by what the camera actually imports (adjoint/importon methods: Peter & Pietrek 1998; visual-importance photon maps). This does NOT apply to direct shadow rays (Inc1), which are already camera-driven — one per visible pixel per light, inherently frustum-local. Recorded here because the instinct ("bias rays toward what the camera sees") is correct but lands on the emission layers, not the shade pass. Note the naive "bias by inverse direct-shadow value" is the WRONG map: direct-shadow ≠ indirect-need (a lit surface can still receive heavy bounce; a shadowed one may need none) — importance must be indirect contribution estimated from the camera, not the direct-visibility term.
- **Shadow compute budget as data.** `ShadowConfig` `[GpuStruct]` (rays-per-light, max shadow ray distance, self-intersection bias epsilon, enable flag, later: soft-shadow sample count / penumbra) — a runtime dial on how much compute the shadow pass spends, sibling to `LightingConfig`. Lands in Inc1 (§4) as the shadow pass's config; area/soft shadows extend it later.
