## LANDABLE NOW

**Generator.** `libraries/ShaderManagement/src/SpirvInterfaceGenerator.cpp` (`GenerateMergedToString`, inside `if (!merged.pushMembers.empty())`) emits `struct PushBlock` for every merged push block:
- Gap pads `uint8_t _padN[...]` come from the merged offsets. Feature-gated members sit under `#if defined(F1) && defined(F2)`. An absent gated slot becomes `#else uint8_t _absentN[size];` when a later member exists. A tail pad is emitted when the block is ungated and shorter than `SIZE`.
- `struct alignas(16) PushBlock` when any member is gated (std430 rounding).
- Per member: `static_assert(offsetof(PushBlock, name) == Push::name::OFFSET, ...)`. Total: `sizeof(PushBlock) == Push::SIZE` for ungated blocks, `<= Push::SIZE` for gated ones.
- Member types come from the new helper `PushMemberCppType` (anonymous namespace). It returns `""` for anything it cannot map exactly, which leaves that member out. It maps Float/Int/UInt vectors to `glm::vecN`/`glm::ivecN`/`glm::uvecN`, and float matrices to `glm::matCxR`.

**Reflector.** `SpirvReflectionData.h` adds `SpirvTypeInfo::componentType`. `SpirvReflector.cpp` sets it for vectors and matrices from `SPV_REFLECT_TYPE_FLAG_INT` and signedness. Without this the first regeneration emitted byte arrays (`uint8_t cameraPos[12]`).
